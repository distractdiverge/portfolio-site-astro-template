# Audience

**Status:** Phase 1 complete. Confirmed by the owner.

## Stated by the owner

- Any potential client
- Anyone in the tech industry who might hire them
- Recruiters and peers in the industry

## Primary reader (confirmed)

**Recruiters and hiring managers**, including people who would hire a fractional CTO or book the owner for technical services. They skim, often on a phone, and have under a minute. They need the answer to "who is this, and can they do what I need?" above the fold.

## Secondary readers (confirmed)

- Peers in the industry
- Others interested in the owner from a professional standpoint

## Core task

**Evaluate.** The visitor decides whether to get in touch. The site should answer three questions quickly:
1. Who is Alex?
2. What can Alex do?
3. What has Alex worked on?

## Desired perception

"Clean, no-nonsense, focused on perfection without clutter." The visitor should feel they understand who Alex is and what Alex can do for them.

## Personality words (from owner)

Fair/balanced, inquisitive, passionate, collaborative, inclusive. Related vocabulary is in [[principles]].

## Accessibility requirement

The site must be accessible and easy to read for people with any type of disability. See [[principles]].
