# Direction

**Status:** Confirmed by the owner. Tokens generated in `tokens/`.

## Confirmed blend

- **Utility, 70%:** functional, honest, no-nonsense, "crisp paper." Matches "clean, no-nonsense, focused on perfection without clutter."
- **Warmth, 30%:** passionate, collaborative, inclusive. Keeps the site from feeling cold.

## How the blend shows up in tokens

- Warm paper tint on the light background (Warmth), with a system font stack and tight, honest hierarchy (Utility).
- Small radii and one soft shadow (Utility), with purple accents and personal imagery (Warmth).
- Subtle motion only (Utility), with the rainbow fern and small icon quirks (Warmth).

## Alternative considered

- **Precision:** sharp and dense. Rejected because the owner wants no dense text.

## Rationale for the blend

The site must feel like a clean document, not a dashboard, and it still has to be warm and personal through photos, quirks, and accent colors.
