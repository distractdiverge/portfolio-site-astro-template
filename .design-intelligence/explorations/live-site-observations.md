# Live site observations: rainbowfern.com

**Captured:** 2026-11-08 with playwright-cli.

**Screenshots:**
- `rainbowfern-desktop-home.png` (1440x900)
- `rainbowfern-desktop-projects.png` (1440x900)
- `rainbowfern-mobile-home.png` (390x844)

## Home page

- White background, black text, slate-gray buttons.
- The self-portrait sits to the right of the intro text on desktop, and to the left of it on mobile.
- Intro copy is short and clear. Good model for the "small chunks" goal.
- "Current Projects" uses three columns on desktop. On mobile they become narrow and dense, with tall stacks of short lines. This conflicts with the "no dense text" rule.
- The Shadow the dog caption appears in the "What I Do" section, below the fold in the capture.

## Projects page

- Hero is a brick-wall photo with a dark overlay and a black button labeled **"Button"**. The label is clearly a leftover placeholder.
- Project card images come from `picsum.photos` (random stock photos: a café table, a grey placeholder). These should be replaced with the owner's own images.
- Project cards use a bordered card with shadow. Simple and readable.

## Colors

- Live site body: `rgb(255, 255, 255)` background, `rgb(0, 0, 0)` text.
- The live site does not show the grayscale + purple palette or the dark theme that the local repo defines. The local repo may be ahead of the deployed site.

## Matches the owner's goals

- Clear hierarchy and short intro copy.
- The personal photo adds warmth.

## Conflicts with the owner's goals

- Stock placeholder images and a "Button" label.
- Dense multi-column text on mobile.
- Brick-wall hero is a stock image, not the owner's nature photography.
